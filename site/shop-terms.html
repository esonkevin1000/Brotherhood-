In short: Brotherhood is a booking and management tool. It helps you track services, earnings and your team. It is not a cash register, POS or accounting system, it does not issue receipts or invoices, and it does not handle your customers’ payments. You stay responsible for your own BIR registration, invoices and taxes.

1. Your account and team

  • Give true details when you sign up and keep them current. Keep your password and your staff PINs private. You are responsible for everything done under your shop’s logins.
  • You decide who on your team gets a login and what each one can open. Remove a login when a person leaves.
  • Each shop has its own separate data. Other shops cannot see your records and you cannot see theirs.

2. Free trial, plans and payment

  • New shops get a free trial. No card is needed. The length of the trial and the monthly price of each plan are shown on the sign-up page and in your back office.
  • After the trial, the service continues when you pay for a plan. You pay us by the methods we show you (for example GCash or bank transfer). We issue our own official invoice for the subscription fee you pay us.
  • If the trial or a paid period ends without payment, your shop becomes view-only: you can still log in, see and export your records, but you cannot add bookings or services until you renew. We do not delete your data because of non-payment for at least 90 days, and we tell you before we do.
  • Prices can change. We tell you at least 30 days before a new price applies to you. Paid periods are not refunded, except where the law requires.

3. Taxes and the BIR

  • Brotherhood is for bookings and tracking only. The amounts you record (finished services, payment method, earnings) are for your own management. Every money screen says it is not an official receipt.
  • You must register your business, issue your own BIR invoices from your registered booklet or system, keep your books, and file and pay your taxes. There is an optional “BIR invoice no.” field so you can note the invoice you issued for a service.

4. Payments from your customers

Customers pay you directly at your shop. Brotherhood does not collect, hold or transfer money for you. If we add online payments in the future, they will run through a licensed payment company under its own terms.

We process this data only for you, only to run the service, and only as you instruct through the app.
