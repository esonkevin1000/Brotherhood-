Privacy Policy

Brotherhood app and website · Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Brotherhood app collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. It follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). By creating an account you confirm that you have read it.

1. Who we are

The Brotherhood app is run by BrotherhoodBarber (the “shop”, “we”). We are the personal information controller for the information described below. Contact us at any Brotherhood branch for anything about your data.

2. What we collect

InformationWhen and why
Name, email address, mobile number, passwordWhen you sign up. To create your account, log you in, and let the shop contact you about a booking. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, never as plain text.
Bookings: barber, branch, service, date and time, notes you type, promo or voucher code usedWhen you book. To reserve your slot, run the shop, send reminders, and apply discounts.
Visit history and loyalty pointsRecorded by the shop when your cut is paid. To show your rewards and tier and to let you redeem points.
Reviews, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, saved itemsOnly if you choose to post or interact. Posts, photos, your name and your profile photo and cover are visible to other members. Your email and phone number are never shown to other members.
Profile photo and cover photoOnly if you add them.
Reports and blocksWhen you report content or block a member, so we can review it and hide it from you.
Notifications and email logsTo show your in-app notifications and to send booking reminders and review requests by email.
Technical dataYour IP address is used briefly to limit abuse (for example too many sign-ups). A login token is saved on your device so you stay signed in.

We do not collect your precise location. If you open the map, your device loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address, as with any web map. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your information.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and your bookings, including waitlist alerts and reminders.
  • To show barber and shop pages, reviews, and the community feed.
  • To calculate rewards and apply promo codes and vouchers.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, hiding content, and pausing online booking for repeated no-shows (a rule set by the shop).
  • To meet legal obligations and to protect the shop and its customers.

4. Who can see it

  • Other members: only what you choose to make public (name, photo, cover, bio, posts, reviews).
  • The shop: the owner, branch staff and the barber you book with can see your booking details, name and phone number. Barbers may keep private notes about your preferred cut.
  • Service providers: our hosting provider stores the data. An email service (Google Gmail or Resend) delivers welcome, reminder, review and promo emails, and receives your email address and the email text for that purpose only.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

5. Partner shops, their staff and promo emails

  • Each shop owns its records. Other barbershops also use Brotherhood. Each one has its own separate data: its bookings, customers, staff and services are kept apart and other shops cannot see them. For those records the shop is the personal information controller and Brotherhood processes the data for the shop, only to run the app. See the Terms for shops.
  • Staff data. Barbers and staff who use the app have their name, login, time in and out, attendance selfies (and location, only if the shop turns it on), services done, commission and pay records stored for their shop. Only the shop owner and the managers they allow can see them.
  • ID photos for discounts. When a customer gets a senior, PWD or student discount, the shop may take a photo of the ID. It is visible only to the shop's logged-in staff and is never shown on the public site.
  • Promo emails. A shop sends news and promos only to customers who said yes to emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also tell the shop to stop. Saying no never affects your bookings, visits or points.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, posts, comments, likes, follows, saved items, favorites, waitlist entries, notifications and unused vouchers are removed. The shop keeps ordinary business records: past bookings and sales (name, phone, service, amount) stay in the shop’s records for accounting and tax purposes, but are no longer linked to an account. Reviews remain as part of the shop’s records but are unlinked from your account. Back-ups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to certain uses, and complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). You can edit your profile in the app, and you can delete your account yourself at any time, in the app (Profile → Account) or on the web page. For anything else, contact us using the details above.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and access to the shop’s tools needs a staff login. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

9. Children

The app is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, tell us and we will delete the account.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: any Brotherhood branch.
