Delete your account

Brotherhood app

You can delete your Brotherhood account here. You can also do it in the app: Profile → Account → Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, profile and cover photos, and your sign-in.
  • Your posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, saved items and favorites.
  • Your notifications, waitlist entries, and any unused reward vouchers and reward points.

What the shop keeps

  • Ordinary business records of past bookings and paid visits (name, phone, service, amount), kept for accounting and tax reasons. They are no longer linked to an account.
  • Reviews you left stay in the shop’s records, unlinked from your account.

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

I understand that my account will be permanently deleted.

Can’t sign in?

Contact any Brotherhood branch from the email or phone number on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm and delete it.
